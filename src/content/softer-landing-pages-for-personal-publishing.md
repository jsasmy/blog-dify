## Less performance, more posture

Many personal websites borrow the body language of products: aggressive calls to action, crowded hero sections, and urgent promises.

But a personal blog can benefit from a different posture. It can slow down, use fewer elements, and create room around the writing.

## Hierarchy is kindness

Gentle design still needs strong hierarchy. Clear section titles, content groupings, and comfortable spacing help readers feel held by the page.

Beautiful restraint is still active design.
