## Lower the startup cost

Most writing systems fail because they assume you will always have large blocks of energy. A resilient system expects interruptions.

I keep a small queue with three states: **seed**, **draft**, and **nearly ready**. Each state has a different energy requirement.

## Reuse structure

When a post format repeats, the mind can focus on the idea instead of the scaffolding. Repeated structures are not boring; they are freeing.

## Leave useful traces

End each session by leaving a note for your future self. Write the next paragraph opening, a reminder about the argument, or the question that still needs an example.
