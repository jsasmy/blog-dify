## Move from accumulation to narrative

A pile of notes is not yet a piece. Readers need sequence, contrast, and signals about what matters.

One reliable approach is to organize notes into three layers:

1. the tension
2. the observation
3. the implication

That sequence helps transform information into meaning.

## Use examples early

Examples keep abstraction from floating away. Even a small concrete case can make the rest of the article easier to follow.
